# Account security

## Resetting your password

To reset your password, open Settings, choose Security, and click Reset password. We email a reset link to the address on your account. The reset link expires after 30 minutes. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder or ask an administrator to send a new link.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a one-time code from an authenticator app at sign-in. Enable 2FA under Settings, Security, Two-factor authentication. Administrators can require 2FA for every member of the organization from the admin console.

## Sessions

Sessions expire after 14 days of inactivity. You can sign out of all devices from Settings, Security, Active sessions.
